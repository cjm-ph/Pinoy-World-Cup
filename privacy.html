Privacy Policy

Pinoy World Cup

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Pinoy World Cup ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, play World Cup tournaments, sign in with Google, or use community features.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Philippine data protection laws, including Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

1. Information We Collect

Information from Google Sign-In

When you choose to sign in using Google, we may receive your email address, display name, Google account identifier, and profile information made available through the authentication process.

You do not need an account to play public tournaments.

Game and Tournament Information

We collect gameplay information such as tournament selections, match results, completed tournaments, chosen champions, and statistics necessary to operate rankings and tournament features.

For signed-in members, verified completed games may be linked to their accounts to support champion badges and related features.

Comments and Community Information

When you use comments, we may process your display name, comment text, submission time, reports, moderation records, and information required to prevent abuse.

Guests may participate using a browser-based identifier. Signed-in members may have their comments associated with their accounts.

Technical Information

We may process cookies, browser identifiers, IP addresses, request information, and security-related activity to maintain the service and prevent misuse.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain tournament gameplay.
  • Authenticate members through Google.
  • Calculate rankings and tournament statistics.
  • Verify completed games and display champion badges.
  • Publish and manage comments.
  • Review reports and enforce community rules.
  • Prevent spam, unauthorized access, and abuse.
  • Investigate technical problems and respond to privacy requests.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

3. Publicly Visible Information

Certain information may be visible to other visitors, including:

  • Your comment display name and comment content.
  • Comment submission time.
  • A verified champion badge, when applicable.
  • Tournament rankings and aggregated statistics.

Your Google account email address is not intended to be displayed publicly as part of the comment feature.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information in public comments.

4. Cookies and Guest Identification

Pinoy World Cup uses cookies and similar browser identifiers for authentication, gameplay continuity, comment ownership, security, and abuse prevention.

Guest comment identifiers may remain in your browser for up to one year. Gameplay-related guest cookies may have shorter lifetimes.

You can manage or remove cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may prevent some features from working correctly.

If you delete or lose your guest identification cookie, we may be unable to verify ownership of comments previously submitted from that browser.

5. Data Retention

We retain information according to its purpose and applicable legal requirements.

Account Information: Generally retained while your account remains active, subject to valid deletion requests and applicable retention obligations.

Gameplay Records: Maintained as needed for tournament statistics, integrity, and service operation.

Member Game Links: Maintained while necessary to verify completed tournaments and support champion badges. These records are not subject to a routine 24-hour deletion period.

Public Comments: Remain available until removed by the author, moderated, or otherwise deleted under applicable procedures.

Deleted Comments: When a comment is deleted, its public content is removed. If the comment was previously reported, a separate report evidence record may remain.

Comment Report Evidence: Retained for 90 days from the report submission date under our moderation policy, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements. Expired records are removed through our retention maintenance process.

Security and Anti-Spam Records: Retained for limited periods appropriate to their security purposes.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups according to applicable infrastructure retention procedures.

6. Comment Moderation and Reports

Visitors may report comments that violate our community rules.

Reports may be reviewed by authorized administrators. We may hide or remove comments and restrict accounts or participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report evidence is stored separately from public comments. Deleting a comment does not necessarily delete its associated report evidence immediately.

We use technical measures such as request limits, duplicate detection, and other anti-spam protections.

7. Third-Party Services

We use third-party infrastructure and authentication services to operate Pinoy World Cup, including:

  • Google: Account authentication.
  • Supabase: Database, authentication-related services, and storage.
  • Render: Website and server hosting.
  • GitHub: Source code management and deployment-related infrastructure.

These providers may process relevant information on our behalf according to their services and applicable agreements.

Data may be processed or stored outside the Philippines. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our service providers.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Philippine data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is processed.
  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion or blocking where applicable.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • File a privacy-related complaint.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity or ownership before processing a request.

9. Data Security

We use authentication controls, restricted database access, server-side permission checks, and other technical measures intended to protect personal information.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

We review security concerns and take appropriate action when necessary.

10. Children's Privacy

Pinoy World Cup is a general entertainment website and is not specifically designed for young children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information requiring removal or additional protection, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, privacy practices, or applicable laws.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice before material changes take effect.
